Bakaloryalanding page

Bakalorya is a programming platform for Egyptian students in the new Bakaloria system. Students take a placement test, start at the level that fits them, and learn one new unit every day. It’s my graduation project, still in progress, and the landing page is done.

I’m the UI/UX designer on the project. The page is in Arabic and opens with a friendly robot guide, then walks through three simple steps, a video, a grid of learning tools, and a clear call to start.

Kattkoty homeredesign challenge

Kattkoty is an Arabic wellbeing app with a chick mascot. Its home screen showed twelve equal cards in one long grid, so nothing felt like the place to start. For the Xperience challenge I rebuilt it to feel warm, personal and easy to scan.

I sorted the twelve features into four groups, added a greeting and a search bar, and gave the mascot a job: it shares a daily quote and has a different look for each group. I edited the chick characters myself.

12 cards, 4 groupsMental health, grow yourself, Islamic content and productivity. People find their thing faster.
A home that says hiIt greets her by name, asks about today’s goals, and puts search at the top.
Mascot with jobsOne chick shares the daily quote, and each group has its own chick: a reader, a doctor, a student with a pencil.
